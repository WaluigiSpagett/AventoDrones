Flight Service Terms & Conditions

Standard Drone Operations Agreement — Avento Drones (Subject to client review)

1. CAA Regulatory Compliance & Pilot Authority: All commercial UAV flights are operated in strict compliance with Civil Aviation Authority (CAA) regulations under GVC / A2 CoC remote pilot qualifications. The remote pilot in command retains final and absolute authority to postpone, pause, or alter any flight mission if airspace hazards, weather conditions, or safety risks arise.

2. Weather Policy & Rescheduling: Drone flight operations are strictly weather-dependent. Rain, heavy precipitation, freezing temperatures, or wind speeds exceeding equipment safety thresholds will prevent flight. If a shoot is postponed due to adverse weather, Avento Drones will reschedule the flight to the next mutually available clear date at no additional fee.

3. Landowner Permissions & Site Access: The client is responsible for securing all necessary landowner permissions and site access rights for the agreed flight location prior to the scheduled shoot date. Avento Drones will conduct pre-flight airspace assessments and notify the client of any restricted airspace clearance requirements.

4. Public Liability Insurance: Avento Drones maintains £1,000,000 (£1M) Public Liability Insurance coverage specifically tailored for UAV commercial flight operations in accordance with UK aviation regulations (EU 785/2004).

5. Copyright & Deliverable Licensing: Under UK copyright law, Avento Drones retains statutory copyright for all captured RAW imagery and video media. Upon receipt of full invoice payment, the client is granted a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use, reproduce, and publish the media for their business marketing, portfolio, website, and social media channels. Avento Drones reserves the right to use media for self-promotional purposes unless a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) is executed prior to the flight.

6. Deliverables & Payment Terms: High-resolution media files are delivered digitally (via cloud download link) within 48 hours of shoot completion. Standard payment terms are 14 days from the date of invoice issuance.
