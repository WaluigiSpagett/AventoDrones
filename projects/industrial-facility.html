Mission Objective & Execution

This commercial flight assignment required high-magnification aerial photography and survey imagery across an expansive industrial campus. The flight plan evaluated structural roof integrity, rooftop installations, and surrounding facility boundaries without disrupting site operations.

Operating strictly under Civil Aviation Authority guidelines, high-resolution RAW stills were captured, color graded, and delivered in ultra-clear WebP/JPEG formats.
